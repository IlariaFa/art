Zone blanche

Documentary radio creation

A notification interrupts a thought. Then another. Zone Blanche begins with this familiar experience of saturation to explore what silently passes through our bodies and spaces: signals, networks, infrastructures and electromagnetic phenomena.

From the body to the city, and then to solar activity, the documentary gradually shifts scales and modes of listening. Moving between narratives, urban drifting, electromagnetic recordings and sound composition, it seeks less to explain the invisible than to make it perceptible.

With the support of ACSR and FACR — Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles.

Portraits arborescents

Digital project

Portraits arborescents is an artistic research project centred on Brussels' remarkable trees and the systems used to observe, measure and represent them.

More broadly, the project questions what happens when a living being becomes data, measurement, category or representation. What are we able to grasp, and what escapes us? By moving this information between scientific, digital and artistic languages, the research explores relationships between living systems and technology, rigour and interpretation, opening up other ways of encountering and listening to the living world.

With the support of the Fédération Wallonie-Bruxelles — creation grant, digital project.
