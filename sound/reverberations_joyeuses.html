Réverbérations joyeuses is a participatory sound and light project developed at the Centre Culturel de Jette in Brussels in 2022.

The project explored relationships between listening, sound, light and projected images through a series of collective experiments.

Developed first as a workshop and later as a performance, the project invited participants to play with sound, light and projection as materials for collective composition.

WORKSHOP

During the workshop, listening became a starting point for experimenting collectively with sound, light and projected images.

Participants explored how these different elements could respond to one another: a sound could suggest a visual movement, a change of light could transform the perception of a space, and projection could become part of a shared composition.

FROM WORKSHOP TO PERFORMANCE

The experiments developed during the workshops gradually became material for a collective performance.

Sound, listening, light and projection were brought together in a shared situation where participants could react, improvise and compose in relation to one another.

The performance extended the workshop process rather than presenting a fixed result: experimentation remained at the centre of the project.
