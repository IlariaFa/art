NEIGHBOURHOOD

Radio Marie Christine is a neighbourhood radio project in Brussels in which I participated as part of a collective and experimental practice of sound, conversation and broadcasting.

Working within this context introduced me to radio not only as a medium, but as a shared space: a way of meeting people, listening, exchanging stories and creating situations together.

The project played an important role in the development of my approach to art as a participatory and collective practice, open to improvisation, experimentation and forms of knowledge produced through encounter.

WHAT THIS EXPERIENCE OPENED

Participating in the radio changed my understanding of artistic practice. It made visible the possibility of creating without separating production from relation, or authorship from collective experience.

Listening, speaking, recording and broadcasting became ways of building temporary communities and of allowing different voices, rhythms and perspectives to coexist.

This experience continues to inform the way I approach workshops, sound practices and collaborative projects today.
