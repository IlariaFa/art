Writings / Editions

Otherkin

2019

This edition explores the Otherkin community through a documentary and speculative approach. In dialogue with their narratives, the work reflects on the concept of kin, online communities and forms of belonging that extend beyond conventional definitions of the human.

The project examines the tension between a desire for connection with nature and life within a highly technological society. It addresses technonatural identities, chosen families and the narrative possibilities of the cyborg.

Moving between research, fiction and philosophy, the edition questions how identities and relationships are constructed and narrated in the digital age.

Bibula Urbana

2018

Bibula Urbana began as a photographic collection focused on the presence of palm trees as symbols within urban space. Through this visual research, the project examines the symbolic charge of the palm: its colonial legacy, the construction of exoticism and the projection of an idealised elsewhere.

From this research emerged a hybrid being, part plant and part human: a cyborg-like creature inspired by the palm tree. Equipped with its own alphabet and language, the figure imagines another form of communication between nature, culture and technological imagination.

The work was presented as an installation at the ERG gallery in Brussels in 2018.

Cabanes

2015

In a park near my home, people build small shelters from branches and materials found on site. Anonymous and temporary, these constructions appear and disappear as the landscape changes.

Cabanes brings together a series of photographs and texts produced around these spontaneous structures. The work looks at them as gestures of play, refuge and contact with the natural environment.

The edition follows their transformations over time, focusing on their fragility and on the quiet poetry of building with what is immediately available.
