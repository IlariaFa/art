WORKSHOP

Building Imaginary Cities

L’Armillaire — Centre Culturel de Jette, Brussels
August 2020

Inspired by excerpts from Italo Calvino’s Invisible Cities, this workshop invited participants to imagine and build their own utopian cities: magical places where water could take on the colours of a rainbow and cars could fly like birds.

The process began with drawings and plans before moving into three-dimensional construction using a variety of materials and supports.

Working with clay and other materials, participants created animals, wells, swings and other elements inhabiting these imaginary environments. The cities gradually became spaces for collective play and storytelling, eventually coming to life through a treasure hunt and board games.
