Bruissellement

2023 — 49′

For more than 40 years, Mariette has been tending a small garden in Brussels. She takes care of the plants and animals, tirelessly repeating the same gestures.

I discover her almost by chance and find myself drawn to the gentleness of the garden. I return regularly to visit Mariette.

Palumma

2019 — 18′

During the final years of my grandmother’s life, her memory gradually faded, leaving me with an intimate question: what becomes of our memories?

The mind reshapes them, shifts their order and alters their contours. Some disappear, leaving irreversible silences, while others persist until they become obsessive.

Une marche

2019 — 22′

This fiction-poem takes us on a walk in which the environment comes alive: stones and plants carry the narrative and inhabit imaginary characters.

It is an inner drift that gradually transforms into a gentle disappearance of mind and body, absorbed by the elements of the landscape.
